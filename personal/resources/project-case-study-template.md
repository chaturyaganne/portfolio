# Project case-study template

A practical starting point from Chaturya Ganne's portfolio. Adapt this structure to your own work. Delete placeholders before publishing.

## One-sentence summary

I built [system] for [user/task] to explore [specific problem].

## Context and scope

- What was difficult before this project?
- Who would use it, and what decision would it support?
- Was this coursework, research, an internship, or an independent project?
- What did you contribute personally? Credit collaborators and tools.

## The system

Show a small architecture diagram. Explain the inputs, transformations, outputs, and where someone interacts with the system. Link to code and a working demo when available.

## Decisions and trade-offs

| Decision | Alternatives considered | Why this choice | Cost or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Model, retrieval method, interface, etc.] | [Alternatives] | [Evidence or rationale] | [Trade-off] |

## Evaluation

- Dataset or task set, source, usage permissions, and split strategy.
- Baseline and why it is meaningful.
- Metric definition and evaluation procedure.
- Results, uncertainty, and failure examples.
- Distinguish measured results from expectations. Do not relabel a benchmark as a business result.

## Reproducibility

Explain setup, dependencies, configuration, data availability, model artifacts, and how to run the evaluation. Keep credentials and private data out of the repository.

## What does not work yet

List limitations, incomplete states, and unsupported use cases. Label simulated data and planned research explicitly.

## Next iteration

State one unresolved question and the smallest useful experiment to answer it.

## References and credits

Link to papers, datasets, libraries, collaborators, and any AI assistance used.
